## Factory benchmark export, 06 November 2026

User requested factory priorities and the complete benchmark. No models were
retrained. `factory_benchmark_report.py` exports the frozen S and S2 evidence to
`results/factory_benchmark/AnchorSpec_Full_Benchmark_20261106.xlsx` and CSVs.
There are 61 configurations, 122 policy rows, 1,586 bearing rows and 4,758
bearing-seed rows. The 14-sheet workbook includes 987 historical comparison
rows from W, V, U and T. Earlier runs remain in the linked historical report
and archive of 260,124 scalar values from 127 source files.

Read `FULL_BENCHMARK_VA_UU_TIEN_NHA_MAY_20261106.md` for the Vietnamese report.
Priority order is a project proposal, not official DENSO acceptance criteria.
Recommend offline augmentation and validation, then advisory diagnosis trials.
Per-fault misses, wrong diagnoses, gain over matched controls, domain shifts,
physical fidelity, unknown faults, resource use and operational value all
matter. Lubrication, contamination and actual factory performance remain gaps.

1_1, seed 11, CPU one thread, batch 120, one warmup and ten timed repeats.

S2 flow 0.0231 s, S2 diffusion 0.0503 s. Excludes fit, loading and classifier;
not Raspberry Pi or factory latency. Raw timings are in sampling_profile.json.

recall recomputed by bearing and class, gain identities and CI bound order,
